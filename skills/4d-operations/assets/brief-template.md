# Operations & Process Improvement brief

Keep only needed sections and replace placeholders.

## Outcome and scope
Who is affected, task/problem, decision, constraints, success, non-goals.

## Evidence
Source pointers, time/version/population, observed facts, duplicate or missing evidence, assumptions and alternatives. Minimize private data.

## Recommendation
Options, trade-offs, chosen next step, unresolved decisions, and expected learning.

## Review and execution
Approver's words/source, conditions and scope; actual actions and checks; what is still draft or unverified.

## Impact follow-up
Outcome measure/denominator, baseline/comparison, guardrails, actual query/report or manual procedure, review date/trigger, owner, result location.

## Results when available
Observation period, results and limitations, continue/adjust/stop decision, next action. A plan is not a completed impact review.
