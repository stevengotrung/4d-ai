# Learning & Development: practice guide

## Observing competencies

If the user explicitly requests 4D developmental assessment, distinguish Define, Dialogue, Discern, and Determine Impact. M1 means with guidance, M2 independent practice, and M3 handling complex situations. These are proposed descriptions of observed behavior, not validated psychometric scores or general labels for a person. Never treat an AI-written answer as proof of the learner's independent competency. Record insufficient evidence when observation is missing.

## Practice design

Define the work task, fictional scenario, staged evidence or realistic constraints, expected reasoning and acceptable alternatives, observation criteria, allowed tools/AI assistance, and feedback method. Ask a subject-matter expert to check accuracy before treating it as authoritative training. Do not force one model answer when several approaches satisfy the work requirements.

## Developmental feedback

Describe observed behavior, its consequence, a specific next practice step, and how progress will be reviewed. Keep unobserved traits and personal judgments out. Use minimal necessary information and permitted storage. Records intended for a learner should not expose another person's private data. Decisions governed by employment policy belong to authorized human processes.

## Worked example (fictional)

**Input:** All new hires completed onboarding, but several still need help completing a task. Some have not received system access. The manager requests a competency ranking.

**Avoid:** Converting course completion or blocked attempts into a ranking of employees.

**Reasoned response:** Separate access and practice opportunities from demonstrated skill. Offer a task-specific rubric and development plan, not a personnel ranking. Observe comparable attempts after prerequisites are available and describe assistance explicitly.

**Example artifact:** Task: complete one standard handoff accurately. Prerequisites: account access and current procedure. Practice: sanitized case with an exception requiring a decision. Evidence: observed steps and explanation, with AI assistance recorded. Feedback: specific demonstrated behavior and one next practice activity; insufficient evidence where no opportunity existed.

**Follow-up:** Follow independent task completion, rework, and coaching time after practice. Record opportunity/access differences; avoid attributing changes solely to training.

These are illustrative outputs, not verified results. This skill uses the [4D Framework](https://4dframework.org/) as a proposed competency model; it is not a certification.
