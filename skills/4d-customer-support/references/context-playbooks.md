# Context playbooks

## Software

Gather the task, observed result, relevant version/environment, and reproduction detail only as needed. Check permissions, configuration, known incidents, and current documentation before deciding it is a bug. If a documented workaround already failed, investigate that failure rather than resending the same instructions.

For a reproducible or evidence-supported defect, hand engineering the expected/observed behavior, sanitized evidence, reproduction conditions, impact, and unknowns. Do not promise a release date. If the existing `4d-ai` skill is installed and the operator authorizes engineering work, it can continue from this record under its proposal/approval rules. Otherwise hand off normally. Support owns customer continuity even when engineering owns implementation.

After a fix, confirm deployment and customer prerequisites. Prepare a follow-up explaining the change and how to try it. Await customer confirmation or reliable task evidence before calling the outcome resolved.

## Retail and commerce

Separate order placement, fulfillment, delivery, return receipt, refund approval, payment submission, and funds received. These are different events. Check the applicable policy and order state. Do not promise refund eligibility, stock availability, or delivery timing merely from a customer request.

Draft the next supported action and explain what is pending. Escalate to fulfillment or finance as appropriate. Sending a refund explanation is not executing a refund; a submitted refund is not proof the money arrived. Keep promised dates tied to verified policy or confirmed service commitments.

## Services and appointments

Check service type, location, time zone, appointment status, and applicable change/cancellation policy when relevant. Display exact dates and time zones when ambiguity matters. Available slots can change; a suggested slot is not a confirmed booking.

Draft alternatives supported by current information, then follow authorization and confirmation procedures before modifying a booking. Escalate availability or policy exceptions to the responsible team. Confirm the updated appointment and any prerequisites, without assuming an unsent draft changed the booking.

## Feature requests and repeat contacts

For a feature request, clarify the job behind it, check existing options, and capture impact without promising delivery. Route to product when investment is needed.

For repeat contact, summarize prior attempts and their outcomes. Identify a new decision, source, or owner that can move the case forward. Do not count a repeated response as resolution. Preserve continuity when another team becomes involved.
