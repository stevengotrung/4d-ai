# Work record

Use only the fields relevant to this case. Keep the record in the existing authorized location; link to restricted evidence rather than copying it. Unknown is an acceptable value. Updating this record does not assign people or authorize actions.

- **Outcome:** affected person, intended result, observable success.
- **Evidence:** source/date/revision; observed facts; hypotheses; unavailable or contradictory evidence.
- **Decision:** original words, author/source, scope, conditions and their status; clearly labeled interpretation.
- **Work:** changes/artifact links; verification results and tested revision/environment; untested paths.
- **Continuation:** next action or decision; proposed/accepted/authorized owner; unresolved dependencies.
- **Closure:** released? reporter notified? user outcome confirmed? evidence for each applicable claim.
- **Impact check:** measure/comparison, source or query, guardrails, owner, time/trigger, result location; planned versus performed.
