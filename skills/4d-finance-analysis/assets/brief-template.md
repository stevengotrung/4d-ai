# Finance & Business Analysis brief

Keep relevant sections only; replace placeholders.

## Decision and scope
Who is affected, task/problem, desired outcome, decision owner, constraints, non-goals.

## Evidence
Source/version/period/population, facts, reported claims, assumptions, missing or contradictory evidence. Protect private data.

## Options and recommendation
Credible alternatives, explicit calculations where relevant, trade-offs, uncertainty, and next useful check.

## Review and handoff
Approval words/source/scope/conditions; approved versus proposed behavior or terms; accepted versus proposed owners; dependencies; actual actions and checks.

## Impact plan and results
Outcome, baseline/comparison, guardrails, reproducible procedure, owner, date/trigger, result location. When available, record actual observations, limits, and continue/adjust/stop decision.
